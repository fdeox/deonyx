Privacy

Deonyx works entirely on your PC. It has no account, shows no ads and keeps no statistics about you.

What stays on your PC

Everything Deonyx sees and does. The windows it darkens are captured and drawn on your own graphics card and are never saved or sent. Your settings live in a file on your PC (%APPDATA%\Deonyx\config.json), and a crash report, if one is ever written, stays next to it until you choose to copy it into a bug report yourself.

What it asks the internet for

Only two files from Deonyx's public GitHub repository: whether a new version is out, and the newest ready settings for well-known apps. These requests carry nothing about you beyond what any web request does (your IP address, seen by GitHub). Turn off General > Updates and Deonyx asks for nothing at all.

Sunset and sunrise

If you darken from sunset to sunrise, the times are worked out on your PC from the city you pick. Your location never leaves your PC.

The Stream Deck plugin and the command line

Both talk only to the Deonyx running on your PC.
